= EVCS Price

Copy of the EVCS Controller ("Force-Charge" and "Surplus Energy Charging") that additionally charges from grid in "Surplus Energy Charging" mode, depending on the grid buy price: no grid charging above an upper price limit, full power at or below a lower price limit and a linear ramp in between. The higher of surplus power and price dependent power is applied.

== Sources of charging power

In "Surplus Energy Charging" mode the car can be charged from three sources, each with its own switch:

Surplus:: Always. Power that would otherwise be fed into the grid. With priority CAR the car takes the surplus before the storage, with priority STORAGE only what the storage cannot take. Surplus may also come from the storage when the energy optimizer exports it.
Storage ("Include storage with foresight"):: Once per quarter-hour, after plugging in or out and after a configuration change, the controller calculates a charging plan over the price horizon (channels `PlanStart`, `PlanEnergy`, `PlanEnergyFromStorage`, `PlanPower` and `PlanReason` for the running quarter-hour). Storage and car are simulated together, quarter-hour by quarter-hour, with the rules of this controller: the storage takes the surplus up to its limits and the car gets what the storage cannot take; below the price limit the car charges from grid along the price ramp; otherwise it fills up to the minimum hardware power as long as the blended price stays below the price limit. The plan uses the prices of the Time-of-Use-Tariff, the predictions of production and unmanaged consumption, the usable capacity (the SoC windows of the ChargeDischargeLimiters, if present) with the "Storage charge efficiency", the schedule of the configured Time-of-Use-Tariff controller (while it delays the discharge the storage delivers nothing to the car, while it charges from grid the car may charge from grid as well) and the grid connection limit of the Meta component, which is shared with the house and the storage. The plan covers the running (or next) block of quarter-hours with production and the night after it. Storage power for the car costs the "Value of surplus power" plus the "Storage loss surcharge" while PV refills it before the end of that block: the storage charging the car displaces always, a discharge only while the storage is above the "Storage target in the evening". Everything else from the storage costs the grid buy price. The plan keeps the storage at its evening target (channel `StorageTargetReachable`): where it would miss the target, the quarter-hours with the smallest PV share are dropped first. `StorageSurplusEnergy` is the energy above the target at the end of the block, with the car. Live, the storage share in the blended price is measured while the car charges: what the storage discharges after the house, up to the car power (channel `StoragePowerForCar`); before the start it is the share of the plan for the running quarter-hour. The measured share is valued at the storage price as far as the plan allows it, the rest like grid power. While the Time-of-Use-Tariff controller blocks the storage right now (channel `StorageDischargeBlocked`), the storage delivers nothing. Without prices or predictions there is no plan and the storage counts like grid.
Grid by price ("Price-based charging"):: Charge from grid depending on the grid buy price: minimum hardware power at the "Price limit", linearly up to the "Full charge power by price" at the "Price limit for full power". A price limit of 0 charges at negative prices only. If the surplus does not reach the minimum hardware power, the missing power is taken from the storage and from grid as long as the blended price of surplus (at "Value of surplus power"), storage and grid power is below the "Price limit". The charge power never exceeds what the grid connection limit leaves after the house and the storage. Switched off, grid power is never taken because of its price: the car charges only when surplus and the storage power of the plan cover the minimum hardware power by themselves, and the price limits have no effect.

== Hysteresis

Every start and stop of a charge process is a contactor cycle of the charging station, so both are confirmed over time:

Start hysteresis:: Charging starts only after it has been allowed (by surplus, storage or price) continuously for this time. A short burst of sun or a single wrong measurement right after a restart does not start the car. This is also the minimum pause after a stop.
Stop hysteresis:: Charging stops only after it has not been allowed continuously for this time. A short cloud or a kettle does not end a charge process, which would then need the start hysteresis again. This is also the minimum charging time.

Changes of the charge power while charging are applied at once, after the response time of the charging station.

== Modbus/TCP

The controller provides a Modbus table (`getModbusSlaveTable`) with its set-points and the storage figures of the charging plan, so that external systems can read the charging decision.
